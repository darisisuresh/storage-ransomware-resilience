TRUST BOUNDARY

Authority is separated by design.

The analytics plane cannot delete immutable recovery points. Production identities cannot administer the isolated validation zone. Promotion requires evidence and operator approval.

  • Architecture, method, and evaluation protocol are documented.
  • Thresholds are workload-specific configuration examples.
  • Exfiltration-only attacks require complementary controls.
  • The manuscript and raw integrity reports are intentionally excluded.
