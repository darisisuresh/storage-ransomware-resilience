TRUST & PRIVACY BOUNDARY

Authority is separated. Claims are bounded.

The analytics plane cannot delete immutable recovery points. Production identities cannot administer the isolated validation zone. Promotion requires evidence and operator approval.

  • The emulator generates numeric telemetry and never modifies user files.
  • Synthetic results do not establish malware-family or production accuracy.
  • Exfiltration-only attacks require complementary controls.
  • The private manuscript, extracted prose, and raw integrity reports are excluded.
